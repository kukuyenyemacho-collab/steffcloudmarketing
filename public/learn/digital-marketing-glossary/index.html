Learn

Digital marketing glossary.

44 essential terms, explained in plain English.

A/B testing
Comparing two versions of an ad, page or message to see which performs better with real users.
AI Overviews
AI-generated summaries Google shows at the top of some search results, citing web sources.
Bounce rate
The share of visitors who leave a page without interacting further. In GA4 this is the inverse of engagement rate.
Call to action (CTA)
The specific next step you ask someone to take, such as “Chat on WhatsApp” or “Book a free audit”.
Citation (local SEO)
Any online mention of your business name, address and phone number, such as a directory listing.
Click-through rate (CTR)
Clicks divided by impressions. A high CTR means your ad or search listing is compelling.
Content calendar
A schedule of what will be published, where and when.
Conversion
A valuable action such as a call, WhatsApp chat, form submission or purchase.
Conversion rate
Conversions divided by visitors or clicks.
Conversion rate optimization (CRO)
Improving pages, offers and flows so a higher share of visitors become leads or customers.
Core Web Vitals
Google’s measurements of loading speed, responsiveness and visual stability.
Cost per click (CPC)
What you pay on average for each ad click.
Cost per lead (CPL)
Total marketing spend divided by the number of leads generated.
Customer acquisition cost (CAC)
Total cost to win one new paying customer.
Customer lifetime value (LTV)
The total profit a customer brings over the whole relationship.
Engagement rate
Interactions (likes, comments, shares, saves) relative to reach or followers.
Entity
A clearly defined thing — such as a business, person or place — that search engines and AI models can recognise and describe.
Funnel
The stages a buyer moves through: awareness, consideration, conversion and loyalty.
GA4
Google Analytics 4, Google’s free analytics platform for websites and apps.
GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
Optimising your brand and content so AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews mention and cite you.
Google Business Profile
Google’s free business listing that appears on Maps and in local search results.
Hook
The first line or first second of content, designed to stop the scroll.
Impressions
The number of times content or an ad was displayed.
Keyword
A word or phrase people type into search engines.
Landing page
A focused page built for one offer and one action, usually used with ads.
Lead
A potential customer who has shown interest, e.g. by messaging, calling or filling a form.
Lead magnet
A free resource or offer exchanged for contact details.
llms.txt
A plain-text file at the root of a website that summarises the site for large language models.
Local SEO
Optimisation to appear in location-based searches and Google Maps results.
Map pack
The block of three local businesses shown with a map in Google results.
Meta Pixel
Tracking code that reports website actions back to Facebook and Instagram ads.
NAP
Name, Address, Phone — business details that should be identical everywhere online.
Organic traffic
Visitors who arrive from unpaid search results.
Pay-per-click (PPC)
Advertising where you pay each time someone clicks your ad.
Reach
The number of unique people who saw your content.
Retargeting
Showing ads to people who already visited your site, watched your videos or engaged with you.
Return on ad spend (ROAS)
Revenue generated divided by ad spend.
Schema markup
Structured data added to web pages that helps search engines and AI understand content.
Search intent
The goal behind a search — to learn, compare or buy.
SEO (Search Engine Optimization)
Improving a website so it ranks higher in unpaid search results.
SERP
Search Engine Results Page.
Spark Ads
TikTok ads that promote existing organic posts.
UGC
User-generated content: authentic content made by customers or creators, often used in ads.
UTM parameters
Tags added to links so analytics tools know which campaign sent the visitor.

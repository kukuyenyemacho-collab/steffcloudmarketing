How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in Nakuru: 12 Questions to Ask

Key takeaway

Pick an agency that reports on leads and sales, puts every account in your name, explains pricing clearly, shows a written plan, and is honest about timelines. Avoid guarantees of “#1 on Google” and contracts that lock you in for a year.

The right digital marketing agency in Nakuru is one that ties its work to your sales, gives you ownership of every account, and reports transparently every month. Nakuru now has many agencies and freelancers. These 12 questions will separate the professionals from the posters.

12 questions to ask before you hire

  1. How will you measure success? The answer should include leads, cost per lead and sales — not only followers and likes.
  2. Who owns the ad accounts, pages and content? It must be you. If an agency runs ads from its own account, you lose your data when you leave.
  3. Can I see a written plan? A real agency explains who you are targeting, on which channels, with what message and budget.
  4. What exactly do I get each month? Number of posts, videos, articles, campaigns, reports and meetings.
  5. How do you track WhatsApp and phone leads? In Kenya, most leads arrive on WhatsApp. If they cannot track it, they cannot optimise it.
  6. What is the contract length? Three months is reasonable to test and learn. Twelve-month lock-ins are a red flag.
  7. Who will actually do the work? Meet the strategist, not just the salesperson.
  8. How fast do you respond? Ask for a typical response time and test it.
  9. Do you understand my customers? Local knowledge — Nakuru CBD vs Milimani vs Lanet, Swahili vs English vs Sheng — matters.
  10. What will you do in the first 30 days? Expect an audit, tracking setup and quick wins.
  11. Do you offer SEO and GEO? Your agency should be preparing you for AI search, not just social media.
  12. What happens if results are poor? Good agencies have a clear review-and-adjust process.

Red flags

  • “We guarantee #1 on Google in two weeks.”
  • Reports full of screenshots of likes, with no leads or sales.
  • Refusing to give you admin access.
  • Very cheap packages that are just reposted stock images.
  • No website, no reviews, no physical location.

What Is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)? How to Get Recommended by ChatGPT & Google AI

Key takeaway

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) is the practice of making your business easy for AI assistants to understand, trust and cite. Focus on consistent brand facts, schema markup, answer-first content, reviews and mentions on trusted sites.

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of optimising your brand and content so that AI assistants — ChatGPT, Google Gemini and AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity and Claude — mention, recommend and cite your business in their answers. If SEO is about ranking in a list, GEO is about being the answer.

Why GEO matters for Kenyan businesses

When a customer asks an AI, “What is the best digital marketing agency in Nakuru?” or “Where can I get a reliable plumber in Naivasha?”, the assistant usually names two to five businesses. There is no page two. Businesses that are clearly described across the web get named; everyone else is invisible.

SEO vs GEO: what is the difference?

SEOGEO
GoalRank in search resultsBe mentioned or cited in AI answers
Main unitWeb pageFacts about your brand (entity)
Key signalsRelevance, links, technical healthClarity, consistency, citations, mentions, structured data
Success metricRankings, clicksShare of AI answers, accuracy of what AI says

The two overlap heavily. Good GEO is built on good SEO.

9 steps to improve your AI visibility

  1. Audit what AI says about you today. Ask several assistants the questions your buyers ask. Note whether you appear and whether the facts are right.
  2. Make your brand facts consistent. Same name, phone, address, services and founding year on your website, Google Business Profile, social bios and directories.
  3. Add schema.org structured data. Organization, LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage and Article markup help machines understand who you are.
  4. Write answer-first content. Start each page with a one- or two-sentence direct answer, then expand. AI systems love quotable, self-contained statements.
  5. Publish comparison and “best” content honestly. Explain who your service is for, what it costs and how it compares.
  6. Earn mentions on trusted sources. Local news sites, industry associations, podcasts and directories all feed AI training and retrieval.
  7. Collect detailed reviews. Reviews that mention the service and the town (“great SEO help in Nakuru”) reinforce your entity.
  8. Let AI crawlers in. Check your robots.txt does not block crawlers such as GPTBot, PerplexityBot or Google-Extended, and consider an llms.txt file summarising your site.
  9. Track it monthly. Use a fixed list of prompts and record how often you are mentioned.

Is GEO just a buzzword?

The name is new; the shift is real. Search engines are blending AI answers into results, and many people now start research inside chat assistants. Businesses that adapt early build an advantage that is hard to copy.
